# Claims Clarity Analysis Report

## NIPO Patent Examination - § 8 Analysis

**Formal Objection:** The application fails to meet the requirements of Norwegian Patents Act § 8 (2) with respect to enablement, clarity and support; consequently the claims, in their present form, cannot be allowed.

The independent claims recite a device whose functional results – e.g. “the first portion of the first filtered signal is 100 % of the first filtered signal” and “the second portion is 0 %” – are described without any structural teaching that would enable a person skilled in the art to achieve those results.  The disclosure relies on result‑oriented language and omits concrete optical parameters (wavelengths, reflectivity, insertion loss), filter designs, splitter specifications and measurement methods.  Under § 8 (2) the invention must be disclosed in a clear and complete manner so that it can be carried out without undue burden; the present description does not satisfy this enablement requirement (see Enablement Results, severity CRITICAL).

In addition, the claims contain extensive functional wording (“configured for”, “one or more”, “first/second/third portion”) and undefined terms (“Cex”, “optical filter”, “splitter”, “intermediate interface”).  The expression “one or more” is used in several claims without an upper limit, rendering the scope indeterminate.  Claims 1, 2‑6, 7, 9 and the placeholder claim 10 therefore lack the clarity demanded by § 8 (2).  The use of percentage‑based functional limits without reference to measurable parameters further aggravates the lack of clarity (Clarity Results, severity CRITICAL for claim 10, severity MODERATE for claims 1, 2‑6, 7, 9).

Support by the description is likewise insufficient.  The specification provides only a high‑level background on OLT coexistence and does not disclose the specific numbered interfaces (11, 21, 31, 41, 81, 82, 83), the detailed optical filter constructions, the splitter configuration or the routing of split signals that are recited in the claims.  Consequently the claims extend beyond the technical teaching disclosed, violating the support requirement of § 8 (2) (Support Results, severity CRITICAL).  The modular arrangement into two separate units and the introduction of a fourth optical filter are not illustrated or described, further breaching the support provision.

Because the deficiencies affect the entire claim set, the claims must be amended to:

1. Provide concrete structural embodiments (material, coating, bandwidth, splitting ratio, loss, connector types) that directly teach how the claimed signal‑portion ratios are achieved;  
2. Replace functional wording with explicit technical features and define all terms used in the claims;  
3. Limit the quantitative scope to ranges that are supported by the description or supply additional embodiments and drawings that disclose the broader architecture; and  
4. Remove or replace the empty placeholder claim 10 with a fully drafted claim.

Until such amendments are made, the claims are not allowable.

**Conclusion:** The application is objected to on the grounds of lack of enablement, lack of clarity and lack of support under Norwegian Patents Act § 8 (2).  The claims must be substantially amended to include detailed structural features, clear definitions and sufficient disclosure before any further examination can proceed.

## Summary

**Examination Decision:** OBJECT

**Risk Level:** HIGH

**Critical Issues:**
- Absence of specific optical parameters and structural details needed for enablement
- Use of functional/result‑oriented language (e.g., "configured to pass", "100% pass") without concrete means
- Claims recite features (numbered interfaces, specific filter/splitter configurations, percentage signal portions) that are not supported by the description
- Overly broad claim scope exceeding the disclosed embodiment

**Recommendations:**
- Amend the claims to include explicit structural features (e.g., filter coating/material, splitter ratio, connector types) and define all numerical parameters
- Replace functional wording with clear, technical language that identifies the means for achieving the claimed effects
- Add detailed embodiments in the specification that disclose the numbered interfaces, intermediate connectors, and the exact optical behavior of each filter and splitter
- Limit the claim scope to the disclosed embodiments or provide additional supporting examples to justify broader coverage
