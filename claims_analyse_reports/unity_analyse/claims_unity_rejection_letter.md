
Example letter:

Conclusion

After a preliminary review of the application, we have found that the claims concern several mutually independent inventions that may not be protected in the same application, ref. Norwegian Patents Act, Section 10. You must therefore limit the application to the group of inventions that you want to be searched and examined. Divisional application may be filed for the other group of inventions.

Certain defects and observations

The application comprises independent inventions, ref. Regulations to the Norwegian Patents Act (Patent Regulations), Section 8, and therefore the application does not comply with the Norwegian Patents Act, Section 10.

The inventions may be grouped as follows:

Group no. 1:

Independent claim 1 including corresponding dependent claims x-x, concerning a system for seismic source.

Group no. 2:

Independent claim 10 including corresponding dependent claims x-x, a  concerning a method of generating a seismic wave. 

The common technical feature of the inventions according to the independent claims in group no. 1 and 2, is «a seismic source». The feature of a seismic source is general knowledge and well known for the skilled person in the technical field, and accordingly the feature «a seismic source» cannot constitute a special technical feature that could link the groups of inventions as to form a single inventive concept.  

Beside the technical feature considered as common between the two groups of inventions, the independent claims in group no. 1 concern technical features of the system for seismic source. Likewise, the independent claims in group no. 2 concern technical features of the method of generating a seismic wave with a specific frequency modulation without any further details of the source.

We conclude that the technical features of the two groups of inventions, besides the above mentioned common technical feature, are different.

The objective technical problem to be solved by the inventions according to the independent claims in group no. 1, is regarded as how to provide a system for seismic source where the source in an effective and controlled manner utilizes an external energy supply.

The objective technical problem to be solved by the inventions according to the independent claims in group no. 2, is regarded as how to provide a method of generating a seismic wave from a source, where the wave has a specific, long range frequency.

The objective technical problems for the two groups of inventions are different, and therefore the technical features in the claims in the two groups cannot constitute corresponding special technical features. Consequently, there is no technical relationship between the inventions in the two groups, and the groups of inventions are not linked so as to form a single general inventive concept.

We conclude that there is no technical relationship between the inventions in group no. 1 and 2, ref. Patent Regulations, Section 8, and they are therefore mutually independent. The application does not comply with the Norwegian Patents Act, Section 10, stating that a patent for two or more mutually independent inventions may not be applied for in the same application. See also “patentretningslinjene (pr.) del C, kap. III, punkt 6.2” Examination Guidelines, Part C, Chapter III, 6.2 (in Norwegian only).

Instructions

You have to limit the application to the group of inventions you want to be searched and examined within the time limit of three (3) months for this office action. If you choose not to respond to this letter, we will search and examine the invention in group no. 1.

We also inform you of the possibility to file a divisional application for the other group of inventions. See Patent Regulations, Chapter 5, and “pr. del C, kap. VI” Examination Guidelines, Part C, Chapter VI. A prescribed application fee must be paid for each new application.
