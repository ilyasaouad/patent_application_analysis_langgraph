# Claims Unity Analysis Report
## Norwegian Patents Act §10 / Patent Regulations §8

**Conclusion:** SINGLE_INVENTION
**Confidence:** HIGH

**Status Reason:** All claims share the same technical contribution – a coexistence device for upgrading an OLT – satisfying the unity requirement under Norwegian Patents Act §10 and Patent Regulations §8

### Claim Grouping

**Group 1:**
- Representative Claims: 1
- Technical Subject Matter: Coexistence device (Cex) with multiple first interfaces, optical filters and splitter for simultaneous transmission of several frequency bands in an upgraded OLT
- Objective Technical Problem: Provide a device that enables coexistence of legacy and next‑generation PON signals over existing OLT infrastructure without loss of service
- Special Technical Features: multiple first interfaces for connecting to OLT and ODNs, arranged first, second and third optical filters with pass/reflect functions, integrated splitter directing split signals to respective interfaces, dual‑unit configuration separating first and second Cex units

### Common Features
- multiple first interfaces
- optical filter arrangement
- splitter coupling
- dual‑unit architecture

### Technical Relationship Analysis
The common features listed above are not generic background elements but constitute a coordinated arrangement that solves the same technical problem across all claims. The specific combination of multiple interfaces, reflective/pass‑through optical filters and the integrated splitter creates a special technical effect – simultaneous handling of different frequency bands – which links the dependent claims to the independent claim and to each other, thereby forming a single general inventive concept.

### Legal Mapping
Under Norwegian Patents Act §10 an application must contain a single invention; Patent Regulations §8 requires that claims share a special technical feature linking them. The identified special technical features satisfy this requirement.

### Recommendation
Proceed with examination on the basis of a single inventive concept and consider grant if novelty and inventive step are met.

### Exemplar Analogies

**T 0533/09:**
- Summary: The Board held that method and apparatus claims formed a single inventive concept when the apparatus provided essential technical means solving the same objective problem as the method.
- Mapping: Analogously, the Cex device (apparatus) and its method of operation share the same technical solution, supporting unity.
