# Empty Claims - Guidelines

**Legal Basis: Norwegian Patents Act (Patentloven) § 8**

According to the Norwegian Patents Act, § 8, second paragraph:
> *"The application shall contain a description of the invention, including drawings where necessary, and an exact statement of the subject matter for which patent protection is sought (patent claims)."*

Furthermore, the Norwegian Industrial Property Office (NIPO) Examination Guidelines strongly stress that the patent claims alone exclusively define the scope of legal protection.

---

### Analytical Assessment
The submitted application documentation has been structurally parsed, but **no patent claims were identified**, neither as a standalone uploaded document nor embedded dynamically within the description text body.

### Conclusion & Required Action
Because patent claims are fundamentally missing, it is legally impossible to determine the intended scope of the invention. Consequently, substantive examination regarding novelty (nyhet) and inventive step (oppfinnelseshøyde) cannot scientifically proceed.
