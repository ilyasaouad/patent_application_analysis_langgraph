# Empty Claims - Rejection Letter Template

The application has certain defects that make it impossible to process. The description of your invention is not clear enough for a person skilled in the art to evaluate which elements are comprised by the invention, or how it functions. The application therefore does not satisfy the condition in the Norwegian Patents Act, Section 8, second paragraph, third sentence: "The description shall be sufficiently clear to enable a person skilled in the art to carry out the invention on the basis thereof".

We consider it not possible to rectify these defects without submitting additional information or making amendments in the application without violating Patents Act, Section 13: "A patent application must not be amended in such a way that protection is claimed for subject matter which was not disclosed in the application at the time it was filed".

In order for your invention to be evaluated, and possibly a patent to be granted, we consider it necessary for you to file a new application with a more complete and clear description of the invention and all its elements. At the same time you must file drawings, if necessary, to illustrate your invention. You also need to file patent claims, and a brief abstract to summarize the invention. You are welcome to call me, if you want me to explain in more detail what must be included in a patent application. Further suggestions for writing a patent application may be found here: https://www.patentstyret.no/en/patent

If you file a new patent application, you must also pay a new application fee. It may be favourable to file the new application as soon as possible, as this will reduce the risk of someone else applying for patent for, or publishing, a similar invention. This could prevent grant of patent for your invention.

If the present application [NNNNNNNN] is not shelved, refused or withdrawn before 18 months have passed from the filing date of the application [or if priority has been claimed, from the date from which priority is claimed], the application is made available to the public. This might be obstructive for your new application, and may prevent you from obtaining a patent for your invention.

If you wish to prevent the present application [NNNNNNNN] from being publicly available, you have two options:

Option 1: If you neither respond to this letter within the time limit [dato], nor resume the processing of the application within four months from the expiration of the said time limit, the patent application will be shelved. The processing of an application which has been shelved for this reason, cannot be resumed.

Option 2: You may withdraw the application. In that case, you must notify us in writing. The application is withdrawn with effect from the day the Norwegian Industrial Property Office receives the notice of withdrawal. The processing of an application which has been withdrawn cannot be resumed.

If an invention is made available to the public prior to the filing of a patent application, a patent cannot be obtained. An invention which has been displayed at an exhibition or published in any other way, may be considered publicly available. If you submit a new patent application, but have made your invention known to others after submitting the present patent application [NNNNNNNN], you should claim priority from the present application. If you have questions about priority, please contact me.

If you do not agree with our assessment that the patent application you have submitted cannot be processed or result in a patent, you are invited to submit a written response within [time limit]. If you do not respond, the application will be shelved.
